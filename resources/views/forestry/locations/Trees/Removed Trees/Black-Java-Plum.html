Black/Java Pulm

Scientific Name: Sizygium cumini
Common Name: Black/Java Pulm
Local Name: Duhat
Description:

Syzygium cumini, often referred to as black plum or duhat, is an evergreen tropical tree known for its sweet, dark fruits. It thrives in tropical climates and is commonly found in the Philippines, particularly in rural areas.

Uses in Filipino Folklore and Other Uses:

Rich in antioxidants, promoting health benefits such as blood sugar regulation and improved digestion. Contains vitamins and minerals beneficial for overall health. The fruit is often linked to local lore emphasizing wealth and abundance, with traditions encouraging the planting of trees for prosperity. Duhat is celebrated during the harvest season, with cultural activities surrounding its collection and use in dishes. Used as a herbal remedy for various conditions, particularly in traditional medicine, to treat ailments like diarrhea and blood pressure issues. The wood is used for fencing and minor construction due to its durability.

Tree Facts:
  • The fruit, which can appear in various colors, including green, purple, and black as it ripens, is known for its astringent yet mildly sweet taste.
  • The tree can reach heights of up to 30 meters and is valued for its wood and fruit.
  • Duhat is often consumed raw or made into jellies and juices.
  • The leaves and bark are traditionally used for medicinal purposes in addressing digestive issues and other ailments.
  • Duhat trees are often considered cultural icons in communities where they grow abundantly.
