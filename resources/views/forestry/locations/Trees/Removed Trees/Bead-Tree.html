Bead Tree

Scientific Name: Adenanthera microsperma
Common Name: Bead tree, Baniklag
Local Name: Kabau
Description:

Adenanthera microsperma, commonly known as the bead tree, is a medium-sized tree native to tropical regions. It grows rapidly and can reach significant heights, with a characteristic appearance of bright red flowers during its blooming season, followed by seed pods that develop into distinct beads.

Uses in Filipino Folklore and Other Uses:

Adenanthera microsperma is noteworthy for its resilience and rapid growth, making it popular for reforestation efforts. Various parts are used in folk medicine to treat ailments. Its seeds are often used in traditional crafts and ceremonial items. Utilized as a shade tree in tropical gardens and public parks.

Tree Facts:
  • Primarily found in tropical Asian regions and has been spread to various other tropical locations.
  • The seeds are often used in jewelry and ornaments due to their attractive appearance.
  • Serves as a shade tree and improves soil fertility through its nitrogen-fixing ability.
  • Valued for its hard timber which is durable in construction.
  • Commonly planted in gardens and parks for its exquisite flowers.
